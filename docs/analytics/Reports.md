# Report Builder

The **Report Builder** compiles activity audits for multiple frequencies (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annual, Custom).

## Available Audits

1.  **Executive Pilot**: Holistical view of all key indexes.
2.  **Reproduction & Clutches**: Track fertility, hatching, and weaning rates.
3.  **Financial Rentability**: Contrast feed, veterinary, and cage investments.
4.  **Sanitary & Health**: Monitor active treatments, healing, and immunization curves.
5.  **Inbreeding & Lineages**: Audit Wright consanguinity coefficient averages.
6.  **Data Quality Index (DQI)**: Identify missing fields in records.
