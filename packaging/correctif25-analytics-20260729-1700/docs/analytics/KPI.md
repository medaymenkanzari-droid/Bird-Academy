# KPI Catalog

Over 80 deterministic metrics are categorized into 7 core ERP categories:

## Categories

### 1. Population
- **Total birds**: Total subjects in the registry.
- **Active birds**: Living birds currently present in the aviary.
- **Deceased birds**: Cumulative mortality volume.
- **Sold birds**: Subjects transferred or traded.
- **Archived birds**: Closed records.
- **Distributions**: Metrics grouped by Species, Breed, and Mutation.
- **Average age**: Theoretical mean age of active birds.

### 2. Reproduction
- **Fertility Rate**: $\text{Fertile eggs} / \text{Total eggs}$.
- **Hatching Rate**: $\text{Hatched eggs} / \text{Fertile eggs}$.
- **Weaning Rate**: $\text{Weaned chicks} / \text{Hatched chicks}$.
- **Clutches, Eggs, and Chicks**: Volume counters.
- **Avg breeding time**: Mean nesting cycle duration (35 days).

### 3. Habitat
- **Occupancy Rate**: $\text{Occupied cages} / \text{Total cages}$.
- **Vacant cages**: Free cage counters.
- **Over-occupied cages**: Cages exceeding maximum capacity.
- **Rotation factor**: Pairs ratio per active cage.
- **Quarantine count**: Isolated birds.

### 4. Finance
- **Total revenue**: Sum of bird sales.
- **Total expenses**: Cost of feed, healthcare, and cages.
- **Net cash-flow**: $\text{Revenue} - \text{Expenses}$.
- **Profit margin**: $\text{Net Cash-Flow} / \text{Revenue}$.
- **Cost per young**: $\text{Total expenses} / \text{Weaned chicks}$.

### 5. Health
- **Disease incidence**: Symptom counts.
- **Active treatments**: Active medical protocols.
- **Medical mortality**: Pathological deceased birds.
- **Recovery rate**: Successful protocol ratios.
- **Vaccination coverage**: Immunized subjects ratio.

### 6. Genetics
- **Average inbreeding**: Mean Wright consanguinity ($F_X$).
- **Diversity Index**: Remaining heterozygosity ratio.
- **Unique founders**: Originating ancestral lines.
- **Rameaux/Branches**: Collateral lineage branches.
- **Patrimoine score**: Synthetic genetic value.

### 7. Data Quality (DQI)
- **Data Quality Index**: Overall file completeness.
- **With photo**: Birds with pictures.
- **Banded**: Birds with ring IDs.
- **Pedigree**: Birds with verified parent IDs.
- **Missing info**: Total missing fields count.
