# Analytics Snapshots

**Snapshots** allow breeders to freeze the complete state of their aviary (80+ KPIs, goals, and scores) at specific times.

## Applications

1.  **Saisonal audits**: Compare breeding season ends (e.g., T3 2025 vs T3 2026).
2.  **Trend analysis**: Track changes in data quality, cash flow, and consanguinity over multiple quarters.
3.  **Local Backup**: Store historical records securely on the device.
